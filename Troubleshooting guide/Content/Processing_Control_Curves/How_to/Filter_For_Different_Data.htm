How to Filter for Different Data

  1. Open Excel application.
  2. Select the "Open" option from MS Office button, and navigate to the folder where the desired curve file is found.

  3. Under the “Files of type” section, from the drop menu select “All Files (*.*)”. Highlight “log_PantherMain_yyyy_mm_dd-#.txt.#” file, and click the “Open” button.

  4. Select “Delimited”, then click on “Next >” button.

  5. Select “Tab”, “Comma” and under "Other", type in "(" and then click “Next >” button.

  6. Select “General”, followed by “Finish” button.

  7. Highlight entire "C" column, then click on the “Text to Column” button.

  8. Select "Tab" and under other option, type "=", then click "Next" button.

  9. Select “General”, followed by "Finish” button.

  10. Click “OK".

  11. Highlight the entire worksheet by clicking on the upper left corner, then click on the "Filter” button.

  12. Under the "C" column, select desired option to filter data.

    Ambience Temp

    Incubator63 Temp

    HPALoadStation Temp

    VacuumPressure

    AmpLoadStation Temp

    Incubator43 Temp

    InputQueueMTUInfo:count

    VacuumCoolingSystem Temp

    Chiller Temp

    Incubator42 Temp

    ReagentCompartment Temp

    		 

  13. Highlight the entire column (previously chosen).

  14. Select the “Insert” tab, click on “Line” button.

  15. Right click on the curve, and select “Move Chart”.

  16. Select “New sheet” and name it as desired.

  17. Edit the graph as desired.

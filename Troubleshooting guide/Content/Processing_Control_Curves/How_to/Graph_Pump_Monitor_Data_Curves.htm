How to Graph Pump Monitor Data Curves

  1. Open Excel application.
  2. Select the "Open" option from MS Office button, and navigate to the folder where the desired curve file is found.

  1. Under the “Files of type” section, from the drop menu select “All Files (*.*)”. Highlight “ProcessControlData_PumpMonitor_yyyy_mm_dd-#.curve” file, and click the “Open” button.

  1. Select “Delimited”, then click on “Next >” button.

  1. Select “Tab”, “Semicolon” delimiters and then click “Next >” button.

  1. Select “General”, followed by “Finish” button.

  1. Scroll to the right until the "Values" column is encountered, and then highlight data..

  1. Select the “Insert” tab, click on “Line” button.

  1. Select the Type of graph desired.

  1. Right click on the curve, and select “Move Chart".

  1. Select "New sheet” and name it as desired.

  1. Edit the graph as desired.

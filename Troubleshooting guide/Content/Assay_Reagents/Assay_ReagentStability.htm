Reagent Onboard Stability Calculation

Onboard Stability Information:

To find onboard stability information:

  1. Find a Barcode which belongs to the reagent kit in question.
  2. Query the logs by reagent barcode.

  3. Depending on the string to inspect… The remaining stability time is in minutes (can also be in ticks) or days.Hours:Minutes:Seconds.

    For the Assay type 48 = CV/TV kit, remaining time reflects a new kit as the PI for CV/TV shows 5 days.

    7200 minutes * (1 hr/60 min) * (1 day/24 hrs) = 5 days.

30 Day Stability Information:

To find the recon expiration you can use the frequently seen UI update logs (e.g. from another set of logs that I was reviewing):

OnUpdate[1620]: updateType=Updated kit=(181,63,04-Feb-21 02:26:43,163,223,Valid,InUse) ARKVI=(181,1,63,SARSCoV2,04-Feb-21 02:26:43,163,223,Valid,InUse)

Technically this date is the closest of the master lot and recon expirations (OBS is not considered) but as you can imagine this will most often be the recon expiration.

I found it easiest to open view log, find the kit expiration error in EventLogEntries, double click the error to find the error in the Low level log, then search that log for: "updateType=Updated kit=(xxx" (xxx= the kit number of interest, in the example below it would be 113).

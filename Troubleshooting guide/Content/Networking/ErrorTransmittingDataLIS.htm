Error Code

Panther does not receive acknowledgements (ACK) from the LIS. Technical Support

Serial and TCP/IP

Reset the LIS Connection in Panther GUI

Interface between Panther and LIS may be locked. To restore functionality, reset the connection.

Resolution

  1. Make sure the instrument is in a Ready or Setup state.

  2. Click Admin in the GUI's top menu.

  3. Click LIS Configuration.

  4. To disable the LIS, uncheck the Enable LIS connection checkbox.

  5. Click Save at the bottom of the screen.

  6. Wait one minute for the connection to reset.

  7. To enable the LIS, check the Enable LIS connection checkbox.

  8. Click Save.

  9. Click Start to restart the LIS connection.

Test

Retransmit a result to the LIS:

  1. Click Results in the top menu bar.

  2. Select a patient result to send to the LIS.

  3. Check Send Results to LIS at the bottom of the screen.

  4. Confirm no errors result while transmtting data to LIS messages.

Instruct Customer to Restart LIS Server

The interface between the Panther and LIS may be locked on the LIS side. To restore functionality, restart the LIS server and application.

Resolution

Customer restarts LIS server and application.

Test

To retransmit a test result to the LIS:

  1. Click Results in the top menu bar.

  2. Select a patient result to send to the LIS.

  3. Click Send results to LIS at the bottom of the screen.

  4. Confirm no errors result while transmtting data to LIS messages.

Serial Only

Reseat Serial Cable or Serial-to-Ethernet Adapter (e.g. Lantronix)

Physical connection may be loose or not connected.

Resolution

Instruct customer to reseat the connection at the workstation serial port. It may be a serial cable, but is more likely a Serial-to-Ethernet Adaptor (e.g. Lantronix).

Test

To retransmit a test result to the LIS:

  1. Click Results in the top menu bar.

  2. Select a patient result to send to the LIS.

  3. Click Send results to LIS at the bottom of the screen.

  4. Confirm no errors result while transmtting data to LIS messages.

Replace Cables (Serial or Ethernet)

Cabling may be broken or defective.

Resolution

Instruct customer to replace cables between workstation and wall port or LIS workstation/server.

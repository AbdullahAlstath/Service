Good Curves

  • Negative Controls are identified by 2814231697150220.
  • Panel A is identified with Panel A.

FAM (RTF1)

  • Negative Control and Panel A should lie flat and should not fluoresce.
  • LPC, Panel B, and Positive Calibrators should fluoresce around the same ttime, in this case between 14 and 15 minutes.
  • HPC should fluoresce with a ttime 3 - 5 minutes earlier.

HEX

  • Negative Controls and Panel A lie flat, and do not fluoresce.
  • LPC, Panel B, and Positive Calibrators should fluoresce with ttimes between 20 and 24. All curves should follow a similar pattern.
  • HPC will fluoresce earlier with ttimes around 17 in this example.

ROX

All samples fluoresce somewhat equally and consistently, as ROX is the Internal Control.

BV and CV/TV

FAM

Positive Controls, Panels A, C and D All do not fluoresce on the FAM channel and their ttime curve is flat.

Negative Controls and Positive Calibrators should fluoresce with a Ttime around 20mins.

Panel B Should flouresce with a ttime around 15 minutes.

BV CV/TV

CY5.5 is the Internal Control RTF. All samples, Panels, Controls, and Calibrators should fluoresce with a ttime around 18-20 minutes.

For Hex

Panel A, Negative Control, and Negative Samples should not fluoresce and lay flat on the graph.

Positive Control, Positive Calibrators, and Positive Sample should fluoresce with a tTime around 18-20 Minutes.

Negative Control, Panel A, and Negative Samples will not fluoresce.

Positive Sample, Positive Control will fluoresce at around 12 minutes

Positive Calibrator will fluoresce around 18 Minutes

TCR too Low

Error Code

262.000.00044

Refer to these sections:

  • Data from Sequence Files

  • Liquid Level too Low in Q1

Data from Sequence Files

Both AC2 (250tk) and TMA-covid use the same fill volume calculation.

Volume 46798, initLL 4592

42206 is the InitLL 1st LLD

42206 volume/ 1884.2 (cross sectional area)= 22.399 ht in mm. * 20steps/mm = 448 steps

The lowest of the retries = 431 steps, so 17 steps below the LL threshold (0.85mm).

The AC2 kit I see from the curve data suggests they are using 100 TK.

As they are running 5.3.2, I do not have access to the coo file.

Resolution

When you reteach:

  1. Grab coo file beforehand.
  2. Use the correct teach tool. The sn suggests they may have a machined tcr carousel.

    • This can cause 40 steps bias in wrong direction.

    • If pipettor z is too high, e.g. needs to be taught lower, then the problem will be fixed after teaching.

Additional troubleshooting

  • Teaching changed by five steps in y-axis and 24 steps in z-axis.

    <<Coordinate_before (tcr too low).coo>>

    <<Coordinate-after.coo>>

  • Different tips were being used – unlikely root cause of tcr too low, but could cause RDV errors.

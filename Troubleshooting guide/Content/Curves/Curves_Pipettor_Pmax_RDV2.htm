Pipettor – Pmax – RDV 2

RDV 2 flag is an overpressure error that is generated when dispense or aspiration pressure exceeds Pmax threshold.

Dispense Side – RDV 2

Figure 1 – RDFA flag with RDV 2 code. Curve Plot - Dispense Pressure - Amplification reagent by Assay type. (1) sort “Color By” field by “Assay” to differentiate processing curves (2) ”Dispense Flags” 0 = successful dispense, no flagging ; 2 = Pmax exceeded. (3) Sort or scan “InstrFlag” field for “Dispense Flag” code. (4) “Pmax” field reports Pmax value of individual process curves.

Figure 2 – Another way to look at data from Figure 1, the Summary plot allows graphing of the specific columns. Notice the other assays there is less variance MTU to MTU (e.g. tip to tip as 1 tip per MTU).

Figure 3 – Same run as Figures 1 and 2, Enzyme dispense for CT/GC only. Notice the InstrFlag column values flips between 0 and 2. For CT/GC assay version 2.6.5, Enzyme Pmax threshold = 4000. For most assays, tips which are made properly should be at least 10% below flagging threshold (e.g. max value in Pmax column for CT/GC – enzyme should around 3800).

Aspiration Side – RDV 2

Figure 4 – Fusion Internal control flagged with RDV 2 during aspiration.

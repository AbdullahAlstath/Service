Customer using multiple PPR tubes and two different recon packs.

Consulted with R&D, and it is spread out in different wells – this looks like leaky vials.

Confirmed with FSEField Service Engineer. that the customer received CTB-00855, and had a bad lot and was still using it.

FSE to go in, and do a deep, deep cleaning.

After cleaning:

Looking at a well by well analysis you can clearly see the ROX channel and to a lesser extent the other channels were reading low as well.

I would suspect that leaking vials over many runs may have left residue that fluoresced in ROX. Sometimes the liquid that leaks out of the tube drips down the fibers and into the fiber reformatter rather than clustering on the top of the well, however it is a little surprising that there were no signs of any crystals or residue.

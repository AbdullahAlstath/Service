Technical Support

  • Locate Tube Handling Robot

    Verify if the Tube handling Robot is over the Waste Chute.

  • If Robot is Over the Waste Chute, have operator check for Tips and Vials jammed in the Waste Chute.

  • Open Fusion Waste Drawer slowly, as Tips are likely to be jammed on top of the Waste Bag.

  • Remove any Tips or Vials from Fusion Waste Drawer area.

  • Ensure the operator has Fusion Waste Bag is fully seated.

  • The Waste Chute will need to be completely empty before attempting to process samples. FSEField Service Engineer. dispatch is likely, due to difficulty of removing all obstructions from the Waste Chute.

  • If the Tube Handling Robot is anywhere other than the Waste Chute:

    Attempt to perform a full system shutdown and restart.

    If initialization passes, have customer run and monitor.

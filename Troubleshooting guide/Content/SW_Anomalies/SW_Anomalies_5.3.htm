2174164 Controls Preliminary analysis of that complaint pointed to an anomaly that happens when the system has a newer kit whose controls will expire soon and an older kit that is not in use. When new controls are loaded on the system, if the controls for the newer kit have expired, the controls will be associated to the newer kit. This anomaly exists in all released SW. Reloading the newer kit    
2467984 EQC Customer loaded 2 sets of EQC. Instrument pipetted 1 pos and 2 neg EQC in one worklist while the other WL only had 1 pos EQC. Developer was asked to look into this (8/2021) This is not very common. If customer wants to avoid this from happening, they can load the second set of EQC after the first set of EQC was pipetted.

692510 Not starting TMA assay while running RT-TMA During assay processing the system brings on two MTUs to be used as “Leading Prime MTUs”. These are used to prime the Mag Wash units prior to them being used, they will also be used to prime the Luminometer if a Standard TMA test has also been scheduled. Since the customer loaded only RT-TMA assays initially, the leading prime MTUs were discarded prior to loading and scheduling of any Trich samples. After the customer loaded the Trich assay, the system could not re-schedule a new leading prime for over an hour. This is what caused the delay in starting Trich sample processing. Ask customer to load TMA assay first, then RT-TMA   Yes 6.0 and up

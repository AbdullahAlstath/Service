Unable to Teach Linear Distributor to Chiller Ramp

When I tried to teach the chiller, I got an error. It touches the tab from the right side successfully, but when it moves down and left to try to contact the pin from underneath, it hits the lower part of the teach pin metal piece.

I tried teaching manually, with no success. When I inspected the chiller, I noticed the pin metal is sticking out a couple of millimeters past the black metal of the chiller slot wall.

If you zoom in where I circled, you can see it sticking out past. When I looked at the old (non-Plus) chiller, that piece is flush.

SSW bug, though, as I guided him over to the tab, it was not there.

He had to go into super – then it appeared.

Logged back into gpservice later, and the tab was present.

Quick overview.

1. Panther side:

a. Applies only to Chiller Ramp and Sample Dispense slot.

2. There are probably two error messages.

a. Unable to detect edge (probably add picture of hook grazing the tab).

b. Detect edge early (use Paul’s picture).

c. A

3. The fix:

a. Initialize LD. *May need to Parameter > set authorization code “1”.

b. Teach > Sledge > select Chiller Ramp > Move Theta, Z, and X.

c. Adapt start position tab (showing RD …)

i. (1) Please check LD

ii. (2) select appropriate position

iii. (3) Get Stored Teach Position “All”

iv. (4) Move Axis to Position “All”

v. (5) add or remove 10 steps in the field and click “move”

vi. (6) Get Current Pos “All” this should match value over in field prior step.

vii. (7) Save Current Axis Position “All”

viii. (8) Reset all Start Positions

ix. (8) Overwrite NVM

d. Go back to Teach, and perform position autoteach for appropriate position.

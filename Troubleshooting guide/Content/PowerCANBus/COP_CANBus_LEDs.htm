LED definitions 1 through 5, from top to bottom. Six-pin CAN Bus sits above power LED.

  • 1 – Power = Green.

  • 2 – System = Heartbeat watch dog.

  • 3 – CAN2 = Flashes only when communicating. Solid = very busy – likely bad connection in line.

  • 4 – CAN1 = Flashes only when communicating. Solid = very busy – likely bad connection in line.

  • 5 – PC comms = Flashes when we send commands from PC to instrument, or from instrument to PC: for example, when service software commands are sent or received.

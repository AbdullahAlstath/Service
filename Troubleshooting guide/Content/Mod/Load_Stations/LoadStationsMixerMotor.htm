AMP Load

097.002.00114

Sample Mix

Complaint Description

Mixer motor did not reach the initialization sensor within the maximum allowed number of steps. For example: 73 21 03 02 72.

05/04/2022 03:56:24 (03 20:56:24)    [D] (Fatal       , High  ) [AmpLoadStation] Mixer motor did not reach the initialization sensor within the maximum allowed number of steps ID=9598 LoadStationMix 'Amp Mix' (Amplification) PlannedStart=03:55:56.287: , start=03:55:56.287 end=03:56:24.487 (MTU=10 BarcodeID=2184243609, samples=77640/68628/Normal, 77641/68629/Normal, 77650/68630/Normal, 77651/68631/Normal, , cmd params: AmpLoadStation  ). Event [0x72.0x02.0x0072] at 03:56:24.387 bytes:  72 21 03 02 72  [114.002.00114]

Resolution

Check for these problems on all three load stations and Mixers:

  • Half seated or not very well seated connectors?

  • Excessive grease on pulley, or on the sensor.

    Image below shows excessive grease:

  • Also, check to see if the cables are under the strain relief:

    Left image shows excessive grease, but correctly routed cables. Right image shows cables incorrectly routed outside of the strain relief.

Load Station Mixer Home Pin Issue

Image shows home pin touching sensor; the pin should pass in the middle of the sensor.

The lower image shows the pin is too short. Load station should be replaced.

Images below indicate a properly manufactured Load station. The pin passes through the middle of the sensor. The pin is the correct length.

Load Station Mixer Pulley Issue

Check for a loose Mixer pulley.

Replace the load station if a loose pulley is discovered.

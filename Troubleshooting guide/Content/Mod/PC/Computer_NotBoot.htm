Panther PC Will Not Boot

Error Code

Dec Hex
N/A N/A

Complaint Description

XE3 Blinking Power Button

Issue: When both instrument and PC are powered down: if the instrument is powered on before the PC, then PC will enter an error state.

Symptom:  Some Fusions with software version 7.1.5 on XE3 will not boot after shutdown.  The workstation power button LED will blink in 2-1 pattern.

TABLE OF CONTENTS

  • Technical Support

  • Field Service

Technical Support

Clearing the error

  1. Power down instrument.

  2. At the UPS, identify and clearly label power cord for PC.

  3. Unplug PC power cord at UPS.

  4. Press and hold PC power button for three seconds to discharge.

  5. Plug PC power back in.

  6. Power on PC, then power on the instrument.

Workaround: When both instrument and PC are off, always turn on PC first.

Field Service

Issue

When both instrument and PC are powered down; if the instrument is powered on before the PC, then PC will enter an error state.

To clear the PC error state, the instrument needs to be powered down.  Unplug PC, press PC power button to discharge.  Plug PC back in.

Resolution

Clearing the error

  1. Power down instrument.

  2. At the UPS, identify and clearly label power cord for PC.

  3. Unplug PC power cord at UPS.

  4. Press and hold PC power button for three seconds to discharge.

  5. Plug PC power back in.

  6. Power on PC, then power on the instrument.

1 If both CAN1 and CAN2 are unplugged from Fusion COP, then no error state.

2 If either CAN1 or CAN2 are plugged in, power is supplied to Fusion COP, and results in error state.

  • USB/Ethernet board has been identified as a root cause. When unplugging from the PC, then powering, allowed the PC to boot.

  • The other root cause has been an improper seating of the power cable into the PC. After unplugging and firmly seating the power cable, the XE3 was able to boot.

  • In general, a good troubleshooting approach would be to unplug connections in the PC that are not required for the PC to boot. For example, I generally unplug the breakout boards and CD/DVD drive leaving only the connection to the hard drive. Then try to boot. Plug one in at a time until the amber light shows.

Boot into windows to install the Shield.

  • Enable gpservice, which is in c:/panther folder.

  • Boot into Panther Shield, Explorer, c:/panther.

  • Click enable gpservice, then use the log off button.

If done correctly, Windows login screen opens.  User password is gpservice.

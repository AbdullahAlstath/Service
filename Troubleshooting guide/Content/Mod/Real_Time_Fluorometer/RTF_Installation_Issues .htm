Real Time Flourometer Installation Issues

Two real time fluorometer (RTF) installation issues have arisen:

  • RTFs Installed in Swapped Locations
  • Incorrect RTF Installed

RTFs Installed in Swapped Locations

During RTF install, FSEField Service Engineer. failed PQ with ebl flags on the calibrators.

It is likely a simple swap of ROX and HEX RTFs in the wrong position; that is why you are seeing the timing shift in the curves.

The FSE should switch the RTFs to the correct position, and verify with the nominal check in SSW.

Another example:

  • RTF 1 and RTF 2 signals rule out magnets and slot issue.
  • Checked P128-P143, and delta is 1440 as expected.
  • RTF FW versions check out.

Any troubleshooting we can recommend for FSE, and parts to have on hand?

Incorrect RTF Installed

FSE was shipped a FAM RTF in a RTF 677 (CY5.5) box. PQ failed because the IC did not light off for the negative control. Notice the FAM and 677 curves look almost identical. FSE pulled the 677 RTF, and PN showed it to actually be a FAM RTF.

Note: Request pictures of all boxes showing label and each RTF serialization.

RTF serial number should not repeat.

This is the IC for the Negatives. It did not light off because the wrong RTF was installed.

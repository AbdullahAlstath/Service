RBETR – Reagent Bottle Empty TER

Assay Processing Flag

Complaint Description

Reagent Bottle Empty TER – The Sample Pipettor has detected the level of TER is at or below the lowest allowable limit. The inventory is set to zero for this kit.

Technical Support

Bottle is Empty

Resolution

If empty, reconstitute a new kit. May be empty due to a spill or leak.

Test

Verify the bottle is not empty.

Bottle Not Seated Completely

Resolution

Ensure the TER bottle is fully seated in the TCR Carousel at the proper location for the loaded reagent rack.

Test

Verify the bottle is seated fully in the TER cup (the bottle was originally loaded at the TER cup, then eventually slipped down to the correct position).

Damaged or Incorrect Tips Used

Resolution

Ensure the tips are the correct type and have not been damaged.

Test

Verify the tips are Eppendorf 1mL Capacitive tips with filters. Verify the tips are not damaged.

Spill On the Tip Drawers

Resolution

If the spill occurred on the tip trays, stop instrument use immediately, as the pipettor arms may already be damaged.

Test

Verify there is no evidence of a spill on the tip trays.

Sample Bay Cover Is Not Installed Correctly

Resolution

Reinstall the Sample Bay Cover correctly.

Test

Verify the Sample Bay cover is installed properly, and the tip does not come into contact with the cover during the TER aspiration (the sample tip can become occluded. If this happens, the barometric Liquid Level Detection (bLLD) of the TER will not be able to sense a state change (outside liquid to inside liquid) and will then drive to the maximum pipettor depth, setting the TER bottle inventory to zero).

Ask If DiTi Was Cleaned or Became Wet Due to Operator Interference

Resolution

If the arm is suspected as having become wet, stop use immediately; further use may cause more damage. NOTE: If the DiTi (Disposable Tip interface) becomes wet, sensitive components in the Pipettors can become irrecoverably damaged.

Check Z-motor Engagement

Resolution

Correct the Z-motor engagement and reteach the pipettor arm to the appropriate modules.

Test

Using the Z-Motor/Pipettor Meshing Adjustment installation procedure (in the Service manual), verify the Z-motor is properly engaged with the pipettor arm.

Verify the DiTi Is Not Wet

Resolution

If the Capacitive level is significantly higher than 11000 (19000 or greater), replace the arm as the DiTi is wet and further use may damage the pressure sensor.

Test

Using the SSW, verify the Capacitive level is approximately 11000 at idle (not touching anything).

DiTi Thumbscrew Is Not Sealing Completely

Resolution

Using the Tightening the Pipettor DiTi Knurl Nut procedure (in the Operator’s manual), tighten DiTi thumbscrew.

Test

Verify the DiTi thumbscrew is tight.

Teaching to TCR Carousel Is Off

Resolution

Using the Auto-Teaching Sample Pipettor procedure (in the Operator’s manual), reteach the Sample Pipettor to the TCR Carousel.

Test

Verify teaching is correct. In the SSW, load a TER bottle onto the TCR Carousel; then pick the tip and move the sample pipettor to the Z-max at TER carousel position 1. The TER bottle should be able to be lifted ~1cm (not pinned against the carousel bottom).

DiTi O-ring Failure

Resolution

If the Pressure Integrity test fails, replace the Pipettor arm (per the Pipettor Arm Removal and Replacement procedure in the Operator’s manual).

Test

Perform the Pipettor Integrity Pressure Integrity test in the SSW (per the Pipettor Integrity Pressure Test procedure in the Operator’s manual) to verify the DiTi (or the O-ring) is not damaged.

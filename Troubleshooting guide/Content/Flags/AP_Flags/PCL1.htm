PCL1 – Auto Detect 1 and PCL2 – Auto Detect 2

Auto Detect Process Control Failure

Complaint Description

Process control failure for Auto Detects.

Note: Refer to AD1 and AD2 – Good Curves vs. Bad Curves > Process Control Data: Luminometer.

Technical Support

Check Auto Detect Bottles in the Universal Fluids Drawer

Check the Auto Detect 1 bottles for PCL1 flags.

Check the Auto Detect 2 bottles for PCL2 flags.

Empty Bottle

Check to make sure there is fluid in the bottles.

Replace the empty Auto Detect bottle. The entire Fluids kit, A or B, must be replaced.

Disconnected Bottle

Verify the quick-connect fitting is connected to the bottle.

Connect the fitting.

Bottle Cap Connectors are crusty or crystalized

Check for crust or crystal formation on bottle cap connectors.

Clean cap connectors with DI water.

Send replacement caps if needed.

Prime the instrument and check for air bubbles in the detect fluid line after prime.

Missing or Defective Bottle Fitting O-Ring

Verify o-rings on the male fitting are intact.

Inform FSEField Service Engineer. if damaged or missing.

Missing, disconnected, or cracked straw inside the bottle

Verify that the straw is present in the bottle cap/fitting assembly and is not loose or cracked.

Send replacement straw assembly.

Refer customer to CTB-00256 for bottle straw replacement procedure.

Review Logs

Review Luminometer Curves in logs. Compare Normal to Abnormal curves. Troubleshoot accordingly. [insert link to curves]

If the site is at an altitude >2000’ (600m) and the Luminometer Curves show pullback approaching 3000, inform the FSE.

Next Steps

Review case history for previous PCL errors. Notify FSE if there were recent cases.

Instruct customer to monitor.

Field Service

Empty Bottle

Verify the AD bottles contain fluid. [insert links to Normal and Air curves]

Resolution:

Replace Auto Detect if necessary.

Test:

Perform Prime in Panther

Disconnected Bottle

Verify the quick-connect fitting is connected to the Auto Detect bottles.

Resolution:

Connect the bottle fitting.

Test:

Perform prime in Panther.

Bottle Cap Connectors are Crusty or Crystalized

Check for crust or crystal formation on the bottle cap connectors.

Resolution:

Clean the caps with DI water.

Replace the connectors if necessary.

Test:

Prime the instrument and check for air bubbles in the detect line after prime.

Missing, disconnected, or cracked straw inside the bottle

Verify that the straw is present in the bottle cap/fitting assembly and is not loose or cracked.

Resolution:

Replace the bottle straw.

Test:

Perform Prime in Panther.

Leaky Fitting, Fluid Line, pump, or valve

Check for leaks at all fittings, fluid lines, the pump, and the solenoid valve in the fluid path (from the Auto Detect bottles to the injector).

Resolution:

Correct the leak. Replace parts as needed.

Test:

Prime lines and verify no leak.

Kinks in the fluid tubing

Inspect all Detect fluid lines for a kink.

Resolution:

Straighten or replace any kinked tubing.

Test:

Perform Prime in Panther.

Faulty OLV Sensor Circuit Board

Plot the OLV sensor traces. If the trace has a near zero signal, the PCB may be faulty. [insert links to Normal and OLV LED Failure curves]

Inspect the PCB for moisture and/or dried fluid deposits.

RESOLUTION:

Replace PCB.

TEST:

Verify prime in Service Software and plot the traces.

Perform Prime in Panther.

Luminometer Injector

Plot the OLV sensor traces and compare against normal. [insert link to Good vs Bad Curves for AD]

Check for kinks in the injector fluid lines.

Check the affected AD pump connections are not under or over tightened.

Check the affected AD pump ports are not cracked.

Verify the OLV sight holes in the injector are clear and free of debris.

Inspect the injector housing for cracks or leaks.

Resolution:

Tighten fittings.

Replace injectors.

Replace pump if found to be defective.

Check screws on Luminometer lid and tighten if necessary.

Replace Luminometer if injector housing is compromised.

Test:

Perform Prime in Panther

Run Flashcheck

Delayed Dispense

Confirm site altitude – if over 2000 ft (600m) elevation pumps are replaced with vacuum sealed pumps at install.

Confirm temperature in lab is within specifications (15°C – 30°C).

Plot OLV sensor traces. (Insert link to Good vs Bad curves for ADs.)

Resolution:

If OLV curve shows the transition from air to liquid or from liquid to air is delayed, replace the pump. (If errors occurring on initial installation and site is at altitude above 2000 ft (600m) – replace both AD pumps.)

Test:

Confirm OLV curves in Service Software are normal.

Perform Prime in Panther.

Run Flashcheck.

A/B Valve on Universal Fluid Drawer

Using Service Software prime the affected Auto Detect into an MTU (default is Bottle A). Check for any abnormality (bubbles, or uneven dispense).

Switch to Bottle B and dispense into a new MTU. Check for any abnormality.

Resolution:

Ensure the solenoid wiring is securely attached to the PCB connector.

Tighten fitting connectors on the valve.

Replace valve if identified as defective.

Test:

Verify prime in Service Software.

Perform Prime in Panther.

Run Flashcheck.

RBET – Reagent Bottle Empty TCR

Assay Processing Flag

Complaint Description

Reagent Bottle Empty TCR – The Sample Pipettor has detected the level of TCR is at or below the lowest allowable limit. The inventory is set to zero for this kit.

Technical Support

Bottle is empty

Resolution

If the bottle is empty, reconstitute a new kit. The bottle may be empty due to a spill or leak.

Test

Verify the bottle is not empty.

Bottle is not seated completely

Resolution

Ensure the TCR bottle is fully seated in the TCR Carousel at the proper location for the loaded reagent rack.

Test

Verify that the bottle is fully seated in the TCR cup.

Damaged or incorrect tips used

Resolution

Ensure the tips are the correct type and have not been damaged.

Test

  1. Verify the tips are the correct type (eppendorf 1 mL Capacitive tips with filters).
  2. Verify the tips are not damaged.

Spill on Tip Drawers

Resolution

If a spill occurred on the Tip Trays, stop the instrument immediately — the Pipettor arms may already be damaged.

Test

Verify there is no evidence of a spill on the Tip Trays.

Sample Bay cover is not installed correctly

Resolution

Reinstall the Sample bay cover correctly.

Test

Verify the sample bay cover is installed properly and the tip does not come into contact with the cover during TCR aspiration.

Note: The sample tip can become occluded. If this happens, the barometric Liquid Level Detection (bLLD) of TCR will not be able to sense a state change (outside liquid to inside liquid) and will then drive to the maximum pipettor depth, setting the TCR bottle inventory to zero).

Ask the Customer if the DiTi was cleaned or became wet due to Operator interference

Resolution

If the arm is suspected as having become wet, stop use immediately as further use may cause more damage.

Test

If the DiTi (Disposable Tip interface) becomes wet, sensitive components in the Pipettors can become irrecoverably damaged.

Field Service

Bottle is empty

Resolution

If the bottle is empty, reconstitute a new kit. The bottle may be empty due to a spill or leak.

Test

Verify the bottle is not empty.

Bottle is not completely seated

Resolution

Ensure the TCR bottle is fully seated in the TCR Carousel at the proper location for the loaded reagent rack.

Test

Verify the bottle is seated fully in the TCR cup (the bottle is originally loaded higher at the TCR cup, then eventually slipped down to the correct position).

Damaged or incorrect tips used

Resolution

Ensure the tips are the correct type and have not been damaged.

Test

  1. Verify the tips are the correct type (eppendorf 1 mL Capacitive tips with filters).
  2. Verify the tips are not damaged.

Resolution

Test

Verify there is no evidence of a spill on the Tip Trays.

Sample Bay cover is not installed correctly

Resolution

Reinstall the Sample bay cover correctly.

Test

Verify the sample bay cover is installed properly and the tip does not come into contact with the cover during TCR aspiration.

Note: The sample tip can become occluded. If this happens, the barometric Liquid Level Detection (bLLD) of TCR will not be able to sense a state change (outside liquid to inside liquid) and will then drive to the maximum pipettor depth, setting the TCR bottle inventory to zero.

Ask the Customer if the DiTi was cleaned or became wet due to Operator interference

Resolution

If the arm is suspected as having become wet, stop use immediately as further use may cause more damage.

Test

If the DiTi (Disposable Tip interface) becomes wet, sensitive components in the Pipettors can become irrecoverably damaged.

Check Z-motor engagement

Resolution

Correct the Z-motor engagement and re-teach the pipettor arm to the appropriate modules.

Test

Using the corresponding Service manual installation procedure (Z-Motor/Pipettor Meshing Adjustment), verify the Z-motor is properly engaged with the pipettor arm.

Verify the DiTi is not wet

Resolution

If the Capacitive level is significantly above 11000 (19000 or greater), replace the arm (as the DiTi is wet) and further use may damage the Pressure sensor.

Test

In the SSW, verify the Capacitive level is approximately 11000 when idle.

DiTi Thumbscrew is not sealing completely

Resolution

Tighten the DiTi thumbscrew.

Test

Verify the DiTi thumbscrew is tight.

Teaching to the TCR Carousel is Off

Resolution

Reteach the Sample Pipettor to the TCR carousel.

Test

Verify the teaching is correct. In the SSW, load a TCR bottle onto the TCR carousel; pick the tip and move the sample pipettor to Z-max at the TCR carousel position 1. The TCR bottle should be able to be lifted about 1cm (not pinned against the TCR carousel bottom).

DiTi O-ring Failure

Resolution

If the Pressure Integrity test fails, replace the arm.

Test

Perform the Pressure Integrity test in the SSW to verify the DiTi (or O-ring) is not damaged.

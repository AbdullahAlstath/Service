RVHA, RVHE, RVHP, RVHSL

Assay Processing Flags

Complaint Description

Topic covers troubleshooting procedures for these Assay Processing Flags:

  • RVHA = Reagent Volume High Amp
  • RVHE = Reagent Volume High Enzyme
  • RVHP = Reagent Volume High Probe
  • RVHSL = Reagent Volume High Select

Technical Support

Questions to Ask Customer

Request Logs

Log Review

  • Check c-LLD, reagent aspiration and dispense curves to determine if the issue is related to the reagents or the instrument.
  • If there are no c-LLD curves for some of the reagents, it is likely instrument is still on a lower SW version using b-LLD instead of c-LLD. See example curves below.

Example Curves

  • RVHSL due to bubbles in selection reagent (not a pipettor issue).
  • Enzyme reagent curves are shown as comparison of a ‘good’ curve.

  • RVHP after tip related errors with instrument still on bLLD (SW 5.2.0.50).

    Since the SW is still on the bLLD level sense, it is behaving much like the pipettor in the Fusion side for Oil. Basically, what happens is, the filter density is higher. This causes the slow response, resulting in lower volume detected. The inventory adjusts lower. Then a normal tip measures the correct height, which is higher than the previous level sense. At this point, the kit is invalidated.

    TSETechnical Service Engineer. recommendation: Dispatch FSEField Service Engineer. to upgrade customer to current SW.

Field Service

Reagent Bay cover

Resolution

Reseat the Reagent Bay cover.

Test

Check if the Reagent Bay cover is properly mounted.

Reference

Enzyme Bottle not properly seated

Resolution

Push the Enzyme bottle down until it is fully seated in the Reagent Rack.

Test

Visually verify the bottle is fully seated in the Reagent Rack.

Enzyme Bottle Cap

Resolution

Inform the Customer to remove the reagent cap prior to Reagent Rack insertion.

Test

Check if the cap has been removed from the reagent bottle.

Excess Fluid

Resolution

Inform the Customer to follow the directions in the appropriate package insert.

Test

Use the Dashboard tool to view the LLD heights.

Air Bubble(s)

Resolution

Pop the bubbles and reinsert the kit.

Test

Verify there are no bubbles present in the Enzyme.

Spill on Tip Tray

Resolution

Discard any tips/tip trays where the spill occurred.

Replace the arm(s) if the frequency is >19000 when idle.

Test

Look for signs of a liquid spill on the tip tray.

Verify the DiTi for both arms are not wet by checking the LLD frequencies - "Pipettors" "Main" "Sensors".

Poor Z-motor Engagement causes excess noise during bLLD event

Resolution

Adjust the Z-motor engagement according to the Service manual.

Test

Verify the sample pipettor Z-motor engagement is correct (1 sheet of paper gap).

Early Trigger

Resolution

Verify the LLD early trigger event occurred.

If the bLLD traces show consistent early trigger in one piston location, replace the arm.

If the "delta Z" exceeds 30 steps, replace the arm.

Test

Use the Dashboard tool to view the LLD heights.

Use the Dashboard to view the aspiration and dispense curves for noise.

Try to replicate the event in the Service Software using the "LLD Right Arm" tab and the "Log" checkbox activated for 15+ cycles.

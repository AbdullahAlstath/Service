RVHTR – Reagent Volume High TER

Blood Bank Assay Processing Flag

Complaint Description

Reagent Volume High TER – The TER Reagent bottle volume is above the expected level. This error invalidates the kit.

Technical Support

TCR Bottle not properly seated

Resolution

Verify the proper seating of the TCR Bottle.

Test

Not applicable.

TCR Bottle Cap

Resolution

Inform the Customer to remove the reagent cap prior to insertion.

Test

Verify the cap has been removed from the reagent bottle.

Excess fluid

Resolution

Inform the Customer to follow the directions in the appropriate package insert.

Test

Check the fluid level.

Air Bubble(s)

Resolution

Pop the bubbles and reinsert the kit.

Test

Check for air bubbles/foam.

Field Service

TER Bottle not properly seated

Resolution

Push the TER bottle down until it is flush with the TER Carousel.

Test

Visually verify the bottle is fully seated in the TER cup.

TER Bottle Cap

Resolution

Inform the Customer to remove the reagent cap prior to insertion.

Test

Verify the cap has been removed from the reagent bottle.

Excess Fluid

Resolution

Inform the Customer to follow the directions in the appropriate package insert.

Test

Use the Dashboard tool to view LLD heights.

Air Bubble(s)

Resolution

Pop the bubbles and reinsert the kit.

Test

Verify there are no bubbles present in the TER.

Spill on Tip Tray

Resolution

1. Discard any tips/tip trays where the spill occurred.

2. Replace the arm(s) if frequency is > 19000 when idle.

Test

Look for signs of a liquid spill on the tip tray.

Verify the DiTi for both arms is not wet by checking the LLD frequencies - "Pipettors Main" "Sensors".

Poor Z-motor Engagement causes excess noise during bLLD event

Resolution

Adjust the Z-motor engagement according to the Service manual.

Test

Verify the sample pipettor Z-motor engagement is correct (1 sheet of paper gap).

Early Trigger

Resolution

Use the Dashboard tool to view the LLD hights.

Use the Dashboard to view the aspiration and dispense curves for noise.

Try to replicate the event in the Service SW using the "LLD Right Arm" tab and the "Log" checkbox activated for 15+ cycles.

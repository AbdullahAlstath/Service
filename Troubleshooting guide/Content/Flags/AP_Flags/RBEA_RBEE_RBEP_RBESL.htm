RBEA, RBEE, RBEP, RBESL

Assay Processing Flags

Complaint Description

Topic covers troubleshooting procedures for these Assay Processing Flags:

  • RBEA – Reagent Bottle Empty Amp – the Amp Reagent bottle is empty.
  • RBEE – Reagent Bottle Empty Enzyme – the Enzyme Reagent bottle is empty.
  • RBEP – Reagent Bottle Empty Probe – the Probe Reagent bottle is empty.
  • RBESL – Reagent Bottle Empty Selection – the Selection Reagent bottle is empty.

Technical Support

Questions for customer

  • How many times was the kit used? The more times kit was loaded/unloaded, the higher the chance reagent was lost in between runs.
  • Was the reagent spilled?
  • How does customer pop bubbles? Recommend to use non-absorptive end of swab.
  • Low lab humidity? (high altitude, cold area)
  • Logs are needed to verify the issue. Ask for logs for every day the kit was used.

How to review logs

Pipettor issue? Check c-LLD curves.

Reagent issue? Check LLDvalues curves.

RBEE in the middle of the run? Enzyme reagent is probably not really empty.

RBEE at end of the run? Enzyme is probably close to empty.

    In the example below, the LLDheight for the new SarsCov2 TMA run is too low (460). The LLDheight should have been around 516 (in most cases, usually above 480 is pretty good).

  • Enzyme volume lost between runs (mixing, warming up, lost in grooves of cap).

    Compare the LLDheight of the last use with the first use the next time kit was used.

  • A lot of MTUs have fewer than 5 samples/MTU.

    Issue Description: The main problem is that for every MTU that gets processed, the total volume withdrawn is the reagent (enzyme =25 uL) plus an additional constant of 33 uL of “waste” volume.

    Depending on the number of samples per MTU, the instrument will pipet: 1 sample/ MTU= 58 uL / 2 samples = 83ul / 3 samples = 108ul / 4 samples= 133ul / 5 samples = 158 ul.

    So for every MTU, the instrument pipets an additional 33 ul, regardless of the number of samples in that MTU.

    If this is done in too many MTU, that will eventually deplete the enzyme and cause RBEE towards the end of the kit.

Which curve to check? Refer to LLD Values, and Tube number column.

For example:

  • 0,1,2,3,4 = 5 samples/ MTU
  • 0,1 = 2 samples/per MTU
  • 0 = 1 sample/MTU

Q & A

Q: There are RBEE towards the end of the run, but the last 2 - 3 samples are valid?

A: Usually that means there was enough enzyme for 2 - 3 samples, but not enough for an entire MTU with five samples.

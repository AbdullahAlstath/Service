262.000.00028

Inventory Management – PC System Message

Description

Auto Detect 1A bottle is empty or not connected. Please check bottle connection.

Technical Support

Possible Root Cause Isolation Procedure Technical Support / Customer Action Reference
  • Empty bottle.
  • Verify that the Auto Detect 1 fluid bottle contains fluid.
  • Install a new Auto Detect 1 fluid bottle.
  • PANTHER System Operator's Manual—Load Universal Fluids
  • Disconnected bottle.
  • Verify that the quick-connect fitting is connected to the Auto Detect 1 fluid bottle.
  • Connect the Auto Detect 1 fluid bottle fitting.
  • Dispense Curve showing Auto Detect 1 Bottle Empty
  • Bottle cap connectors crusty or crystallized.
  • Check for crust or crystal formation on bottle cap connectors.
  • Clean cap connectors with DI water or replace.
  • Prime the instrument and check for air bubbles in the detect line after the prime.

N/A
  • Missing, disconnected, or cracked aspirator tube (straw) inside bottle.
  • Verify that the aspirator tube is present in the bottle cap/fitting assembly and is not cracked.
  • Replace the aspirator tube in the cap assembly or order a new cap assembly.

N/A

Field Service

Possible Root Cause Isolation Procedure Service Action Reference
  • Empty bottle.
  • Verify that the Auto Detect 1 fluid bottle contains fluid.
  • Install a new Auto Detect 1 fluid bottle.
  • PANTHER System Operator's Manual—Load Universal Fluids
  • Disconnected bottle.
  • Verify that the quick-connect fitting is connected to the Auto Detect 1 fluid bottle.
  • Connect the Auto Detect 1 fluid bottle fitting.
  • Dispense Curve showing Auto Detect 1 Bottle Empty
  • Bottle cap connectors crusty or crystallized.
  • Check for crust or crystal formation on bottle cap connectors.
  • Clean cap connectors with DI water or replace.
  • Prime the instrument and check for air bubbles in the detect line after the prime.

N/A
  • Missing, disconnected, or cracked aspirator tube (straw) inside bottle.
  • Verify that the aspirator tube is present in the bottle cap/fitting assembly and is not cracked.
  • Replace the aspirator tube in the cap assembly or order a new cap assembly.

N/A
  • Leaky fitting, fluid line, pump, or valve.
  • Check for leaks at all fittings, fluid lines, the pump, and the solenoid valve in the fluid path (from the Auto Detect bottle to the injector).
  • Correct the leak.

N/A
  • Kinked fluid line.
  • Inspect all Detect fluid lines for a kink.
  1. Remove any kinks.
  2. Verify that the fluid line is not leaking at the kink location.

N/A
  • Faulty OLV Sensor circuit board
  1. Plot the OLV snesor traces.
  2. If the trace has a near zero signal, then the PCB may be faulty.
  3. Also, inspect the PCB for moisture and/or dried fluid deposits.
  1. Replace the PCB.
  • PANTHER System Service Manual—Luminometer OLV Sensor Removal and Replacement
  • Auto Detect 1 Injector
  1. Check for kinks in the Auto Detect 1 injector fluid lines.
  2. Inspect the injector housing for leaks.
  3. Verify that the OLV sight holes in the injector are clear and free of debris.
  1. Replace the injector.
  • PANTHER System Service Manual—Luminometer Injector Removal and Replacement
  • Faulty solenoid valve.
  • Verify that the solenoid valve is properly switching between bottle A and B.
  1. Ensure that the solenoid wiring is securely attached to the PCB connector.
  2. Replace the solenoid.
  • PANTHER System Service Manual—Solenoid Valve Removal and Replacement

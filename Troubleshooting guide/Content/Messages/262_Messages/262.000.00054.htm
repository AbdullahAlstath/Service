Technical Support

Possible Root Cause:

  • Empty bleach or deactivation bottle
  • Loose fitting and/or straw
  • Kinked tubing (not very common)
  • Missing or worn O-ring
  • Cracked Deactivation fluid cap/fitting
  • Old, worn or incorrectly installed peristalitic pump tubing
  • Peristaltic pump, A/B Solenoid valve

TS Troubleshooting:

While receiving these messages, when you empty undeactivated waste, dispose of waste as follows:

Double bag solid waste.

Add an equal volume of bleach to removable liquid waste bottle, and let it sit for one hour prior to discarding.

  • Verify bleach and both deactivation bottles have enough fluid
  • Verify quick-disconnect is securely connected ( inspect the O-ring)
  • Verify the straw inside deactivation buffer bottles is snug
  • Check the tubing for any air bubbles (near bottle or near peripump?)
  • Obtain logs to review
  • Analyze OLV dispense curves (see https://teams.hologic.com/sites/MolecularTSDx/KnowledgeBase/Panther/Topics.aspx)

Field Service

Note: Please refer to Output Queue Curves for Process Control Analysis.

Peri Pump Tubing

Aged or worn peristaltic pump tubing

Resolution:

Replace the Peristaltic Pump tubing

Test:

  • Plot the OLV sensor traces.
    • If the trace shows that no fluid is being dispensed, then replace the tubing. Also, inspect the fluid line that routes from the Universal Fluid drawer to the Peristaltic pump.
    • If the fluid line is not primed from the drawer to the pump, then fluid may be leaking past the pump due to worn tubing.

Peri Pump Tubing Clips

Improperly installed Peristaltic Pump back plate or tubing mount clips.

Resolution:

Reinstall the clips correctly

Test:

  1. Verify that peristaltic pump back plates are properly installed. Verify that the tubing mount clips are properly installed in the peristaltic pump.
  2. Also make sure the tubing fittings are 5mm instead of 7mm long.

Leak

Leaky fitting, fluid line, pump, or valve

Resolution:

Correct the Leak

Test:

Check for leaks at all fittings, fluid lines, the pump, and the solenoid valve in the fluid path (from the Bleach and Deactivation Buffer bottles to the injector).

Kinked Fluid Line

Resolution:

  1. Remove the kink.
  2. Verify that the fluid line is not leaking at the kink location.

Test:

Inspect all Peristaltic Pump fluid lines for kinks.

Faulty OLV Sensor Circuit Board

Resolution:

Replace the PCB

Test:

  • Plot the OLV sensor traces.
    • If the trace has a near zero signal, then the PCB may be faulty. Also, inspect the PCB for moisture and/or dried fluid deposits

Output Queue Injector

Resolution:

Replace the Injector

Test:

  • Check for kinks in Detect injector fluid lines. Inspect the injector housing for leaks.
  • Verify that the OLV sight holes in the injector are clear and free of debris.

Faulty Peri Pump

RESOLUTION:

Replace the Peristaltic pump.

Test:

Check that the Peristaltic Pump is functioning properly.

Continuous Access Waste

Bleach and/or deactivation are not dispensing correctly.

  • The sensor does not detect that bleach/deactivation dispenses correctly.

Note: Customers have only one removable bottle, so they will need to let assay processing finish before changing the waste.

RESOLUTION No Waste to Drain:

  1. Let assay processing finish before changing the waste.
  2. Perform normal troubleshooting, such as double bagging solid waste and adding bleach to liquid waste for one hour.

RESOLUTION Waste to Drain:

  1. The instrument will automatically stop pumping waste to the drain. Customer will need to change liquid waste manually.
  2. Let assay processing finish before changing the waste.
  3. Perform normal troubleshooting, such as double bagging solid waste and adding bleach to liquid waste for one hour.

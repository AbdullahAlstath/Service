Bleach bottle is empty or not connected

Error Code

Dec Hex

262.000.00036

 

Complaint Description

Bleach bottle is empty or not connected.

Technical Support

Possible root cause

  • Empty bottle or disconnected

  • Missing or worn connector (O-ring is not a standalone part)

  • Stuck float

  • PCB

  • Worn or old Peri-pump tubing

  • Peri-pump tubing clamp missing or not secured

Troubleshooting

  • Verify bleach bottle has enough fluids

  • Verify the connector snaps in and is intact

  • Check the tubings for any bubbles- if the answer is yes, how far do the bubbles go

  • Verify the floaty is not stuck-by adding DI water to the bottle and see if it moves at all

  • If bleach is not dispensing and there are undeactivated waste errors, remind the customer to double bag solid waste and add equal volume of undiluted bleach to liquid waste, let sit for at least 30 min before discarding.

  • Verify the bottle status panel light is green. If is not, reset instrument. If this doesn't help, dispatch FSEField Service Engineer..

  • Find out when the last PM or CCC was done to replace the peri-pump tubing. If it was recent, ask customer to check peri-pump tubing housing is properly seated.

Field Service

Aged or Worn Peristaltic Pump Tubing

Resolution

Replace the Peristaltic Pump Tubing

Test

Plot the OLV sensor traces. If the trace shows that no fluid is being dispensed, then replace the tubing. Also, inspect the fluid line that routes from the Universal Fluid drawer to the Peristaltic pump. If the fluid line is not primed from the drawer to the pump, then fluid may be leaking past the pump due to worn tubing.

Improperly Installed Peristaltic Pump Back Plate or Tubing Mount Clips

Resolution

Install the components correctly.

Test

Verify that peristaltic pump back plates are properly installed. Verify that the tubing mount clips are properly installed in the peristaltic pump

Leaky Fitting, Fluid Line, Pump, or Valve

Resolution

Correct the Leak

Test

Check for leaks at all fittings, fluid lines, the pump, and the solenoid valve in the fluid path (from the bleach bottle to the injector)

Kinked Fluid Line

Resolution

Remove the kink. Verify that the fluid line is not leaking at the kink location.

Test

Check for leaks at all fittings, fluid lines, the pump, and the solenoid valve in the fluid path (from the bleach bottle to the injector)

Faulty OLV Sensor Circuit Board

Resolution

Replace the OLV

Test

Plot the OLV sensor traces. If the trace has a near zero signal, then the PCB may be faulty. Also, inspect the PCB for moisture and/or dried fluid deposits

Output Queue Injector

Resolution

Replace the Injector

Test

Check for kinks in Detect injector fluid lines. Inspect the injector housing for leaks. Verify that the OLV sight holes in the injector are clear and free of debris.

Bleach Bottle full Sensor

Resolution

  1. Connect sensor
  2. Clean sensor
  3. Adjust sensor to get it closer to bleach bottle floater
  4. Replace Faulty sensor

Test

  1. Verify the sensor is connected
  2. Using Service Software, verify the sensor properly reads
  3. Faulty Sensor or broken wires

Faulty Peristaltic Pump

Resolution

Replace the pump as needed

Test

Check for proper operation of the Peristaltic Pump in SSW.

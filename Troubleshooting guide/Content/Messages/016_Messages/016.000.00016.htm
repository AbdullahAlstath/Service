Technical Support

Troubleshooting

  1. This error will occur again, however frequency between failures is not predictable. Review case history to see if linear distributor errors occurred recently. Dispatch FSEField Service Engineer. sev 2 or sev 3.

  2. Instruct customer to shutdown/restart, prime, and perform a run of untested samples.

  3. If the position is tested during a MW clean, instruct customer to perform the MW clean maintenance.

    1. Reference the “LD Positions Tested During Prime or MW” sheet below.

  4. Review logs for linear distributor messages, warnings, and critical error messages before the fatal error message.

    1. NotePad++ Search Terms:

    1. All LD movements: COP->PC 10

    2. All LD movements that gave a warning, critical, or fatal error message: COP->PC 10 [0-9][0-9] [0][1-3]

Field Service

Bad Distributor Flex Cable

Resolution

Replace faulty flex cable.

Note: If unable to determine which cable is damaged, replace both X and Theta cables.

Test

  1. Look through the logs and determine the location at which the Distributor error occurred.
  2. Using Service Software, cycle the Distributor to the faulty location to attempt to recreate the error.

Poor Connection Between Distributor and the COP

Resolution:

Properly plug in all cables.

Test

  1. Check to see if the Distributor–COP cable is properly plugged in.
  2. Check to see if all other Distributor cables (flex cables, motor cables, etc.) are properly plugged in.

Distributor Jammed Against Another Module

Resolution

Clear the jam.

Test

  1. Power down the instrument.
  2. Look into the instrument from the left side or open the Service Drawer.
  3. Check to see if the Distributor is obviously jammed against another module.
  4. If it is, determine why the Distributor is jammed. Is the teaching bad? Is there a stray tiplet blocking the Distributor?

Alignment of Distributor to the MTU Scanner

Resolution

  1. Check the alignment of the input queue barcode scanner to the MTU barcode in service software, adjust such that the barcode laser is in the center of the MTU barcode.

    or

  2. Replace the faulty scanner.

Test

  1. Look through the logs and determine the location at which the Distributor error occurred.
  2. If the distributor was moving an MTU from the Input queue to either the sample dispense slot or the transition incubator (Priming MTU). Check for messages related to Input Queue Barcode scanner timeouts, it may be a normal message.
  3. If the distributor was moving an MTU to the Luminometer, check for Input Queue Barcode scanner timeouts.
  4. Using service software, repeatedly scan MTUs and visually check the laser alignment to the MTU barcode.

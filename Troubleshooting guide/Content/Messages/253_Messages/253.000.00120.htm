Pipettor X-Axis Motor Overload Detected

Error Codes

Dec Hex Arm
253.000.00120 0.FDx0.00x0.78 Sample
253.000.00123 0.FDx0.00x0.7B Reagent
253.014.00120 0.FDx0.0Ex0.78 Sample
253.015.00123 0.FDx0.0Fx0.7B Reagent

Complaint Description

Either Reagent or Sample Panther Pipettor X-axis motor overload detected.

Technical Support

    • While instrument is off, open canopy. Check for and remove any obstructions.

      • Inspect sample bay shield.

      • Remove and replace.

      • Reset instrument.

    • Make sure sample bay shield and blue partition wall are properly seated.

  • Perform a MW cleaning to test sample pipettor.

Field Service

Physical Obstruction

Make sure there are no obstructions present along the travel path ie hoses, cables, tubing, covers, shield etc..

Resolution: Remove obstruction (blue partition wall and sample bay shield), inspect mounting bracket is not cracked or broken.

Dirty Rails

Turn off instrument and slide the pipettor in the X-direction to detect any binding

Resolution: Using a towel, wipe down X-axis bearing rods to remove any dirt, or grime.

Cables and Connections

Make sure all cables are properly connected.

Resolution: Re-seat encoder and motor cables and connectors.

Check for any worn out or damaged cables.

Resolution: Replace worn out or damaged Cables. Verify cables are properly secured with cable clamps.

Belts: Loose, Worn Out, or Wrong Pitch

Using Service Software, cycle pipettor to different positions or randomly move pipettor trying to replicate error

Resolution: Re-tension or replace the belts.

Motor Encoder

Dirty Motor Encoder

Resolution: Clean or replace encoder refer to TB-01691.

Listen for Chattering from inside the motor/encoder, signs that its struggling to keep its current position.

Resolution: Replace the X-motor, procedure is in the Panther Service Manual.

257.000.00003

Manual Action – PC System Message

Description

Requested operation %1 is currently blocked by executing another command.

Remove all racks and close all drawers and doors in order to continue initialization.

Technical Support

Possible Root Cause Isolation Procedure Technical Support / Customer Action Reference
  • Reagent or Sample Racks have been left in the instrument after a system reboot.
  • Check the Reagent and Sample Bays for racks.
  • Ask the operator to remove any racks left on the instrument.
 N/A

Field Service

Possible Root Cause Isolation Procedure Service Action Reference
  • Missing or broken spring on the Waste Drawer locking mechanism, preventing the locking mechanism from closing properly.
  1. Open the left instrument side panel and inspect the locking mechanism.
  2. Open and close the Waste Drawer to visually inspect the action of the locking mechanism.
  3. Determine if the mechanism is properly engaging when the drawer is fully closed.
  • Replace the spring if it is missing or broken.

N/A
  • The clip that holds the Waste Drawer closed is broken and the drawer does not stay fully closed when the operator closes the drawer.
  1. Open the left instrument side panel and inspect behind the Waste Drawer for debris.
  2. Inspect the end of each of the slide rails where the retaining clips are located.
  3. Open and close the Waste Drawer to visually inspect that the drawer if the drawer fully closes and stays in place when closed.
  • If broken rail retaining clips are determined to be the cause of the problem, replace the slide rail(s).

N/A
  • The ribbon cable connectors on the Cooling Module or the interlock are improperly seated or the cable is damaged.
  1. Inspect the connectors at the Cooling Module and at the interlock for proper seating.
  2. Inspect the ribbon cable for damage.
  3. Using Service Software, test interlock reading and function.

Note—If the connector is upside down, the Cooling Module will not initialize and the green light on the Cooling Module will not be lit.
  1. Reseat the connectors at the interlock and the Cooling Module PCB.
  2. Inspect the ribbon cable that runs from the Cooling Module PCB to the interlock for nicks, especially if the ribbon cable is not contained in the cable race at the back wall of the instrument.
  3. Replace the cable if it is damaged.

N/A
  • Sensor on interlock or interlock pin is obstructed by fluid or debris.
  • Inspect the interlock and locking mechanism for debris or evidence of fluid leakage.
  1. Remove the locking mechanism and clean with 70% EtOH.
  2. Inspect the action of the solenoid pin and locking mechanism by hand.
  3. If dried fluid was the cause of the failure, stickiness of the mechanism and the interlock pin movement will be improved by cleaning.
  4. Allow the mechanism and the interlock to dry and reinstall.
  5. Using Service Software, verify that the interlock functions properly, and the drawer closes and locks properly

N/A
  • Interlock damaged or failed.
  • After all other options have been attempted to fix the interlock problem and there is no response from the interlock and the problem isn't due to a damaged ribbon cable. Test with a new interlock.
  1. Remove the locking mechanism and replace the interlock.
  2. Using Service Software, test the new interlock.
  3. If the problem does not recur with the new interlock, the part may have failed and a replacement was required.

Technical Support

Troubleshooting

  • Check lab temperature.
  • Check the time since exited power save. Incubator temperature should lock after thirty minutes of warm-up.
  • Make sure the instrument is not in direct sunlight.
  • Make sure there are no objects blocking vents.
  • Restart the instrument.
  • If the incubator temperature doesn’t come back into range, notify FSEField Service Engineer..
  • If Incubator 1 temperature is out of range (too low) during MW cleaning: could be due to cold endozyme. Recommend to monitor.

Possible Root Cause

  • Lab temperature outside range
  • Failed PCB
  • Failed incubator
  • For Incubator 1 only: cold endozyme (= Magwash cleaning solution)

Field Service

Laboratory Temperature

  1. Ensure laboratory room temperature is 15 – 30˚ Celsius.
  2. Verify instrument is not in direct sunlight - close window blinds, if applicable.
  3. Verify instrument has proper service clearance on the left and right, with no foreign objects blocking the air vents on either side.

Resolution

  • Ask operator to set laboratory temp to 15 – 30˚ Celsius.
  • Close blinds (if applicable).
  • Remove foreign objects that may impede airflow from either side of instrument.

Test

  • Ensure the system resets without error in System Software.
  • Ensure Incubator temperatures eventually stabilize and lock:
    ModuleTemp (Range)
    High Temp (Inc. 1)64°C (+/- 0.5°C)
    Transition (Inc. 2)43.7°C (+1.0° / -0.5°C)
    AMP (Inc. 3)42.7°C (+/- 0.3°C)

Incubator Main PCB

  1. Remove Mid Bay Cover to expose the Incubator Main PCB(s).
  2. Inspect the PCB for signs of electronic damage.

Resolution

If damage is observed, replace the Incubator Main PCB.

TEST:

  • Ensure the system resets without error in System Software.
  • Ensure Incubator temperatures eventually stabilize and lock:

    • Module Temp (Range)
    High Temp (Inc. 1) 64°C (+/- 0.5°C)
    Transition (Inc. 2) 43.7°C (+1.0° / -0.5°C)
    AMP (Inc. 3) 42.7°C (+/- 0.3°C)

See Panther Service Manual for required replacement verifications.

Incubator Heater Foils and Connections

Troubleshooting:

  1. Remove the Mid Bay Cover to inspect Incubator PCB connections.

  2. Inspect the following:

    Note: The labeling on the Incubator PCB is counterintuitive. The left connector is labeled Heat Housing, and is connected to the Bottom Heater. The right connector is labeled Heat Bottom, and is connected to the Side Heater.

    1. Connect Bottom Heater foil (x1), and Switch A to Heat Housing.
    2. Connect Side Heater foils (x2), and Switch B to Heat Bottom.

  3. If all connectors are correctly and securely seated, then move to step 4.

  4. Test the resistance for both Heaters by following these steps:

    1. Turn the power off to the instrument.

    2. Unplug the Bottom Heater connector.

    3. On the connector, using a multimeter, test the resistance for each of the following combinations:

      Test Lead Combination Expected Resistance (Ohms)

      A

      Blue to Orange 1

      10-20

      B

      Blue to Orange 2

      10-20

      C

      Orange 1 to Orange 2

      20-30*

    * This expected value will be additive of tests A and B. So, for example, if Test A was 13 Ohms, and Test B was 14 Ohms, the expected resistance for Test C would be ~27 Ohms.

    Note: On all heater foils, the Blue wire is (-) and Orange wire is (+).

  1. Plug the connector back in.
  1. Repeat steps A-D for the Side Heater.

  1. Diagnosis:

    a. Bottom Heater: Low ohm reading for Tests A and / or B indicates Bad Bottom Heater.

    This will also often show up in the logs or error messages as an IC Failure.

    b. Side Heater: High ohm reading for Tests A and / or B indicates Bad Side Heater.

    This will also often manifest as the Incubator being able to initialize but will never reach thermal lock. The Incubator temperature will be ~1-2 degree below the set temperature for the incubator.

    Example: HT incubator settles in at about 62 - 63 degrees C.

Resolution

  • Correct any incorrect connections as required.

  • If either of the heater foils fail the resistance test in step 4 above, replace entire incubator.

Test

  • Ensure the system resets without error in System Software.

  • Ensure Incubator temperatures eventually stabilize and lock:

    Module Temp (Range)
    High Temp (Inc. 1) 64°C (+/- 0.5°C)
    Transition (Inc. 2) 43.7°C (+1.0° / -0.5°C)
    AMP (Inc. 3) 42.7°C (+/- 0.3°C)

See Panther Service Manual for required replacement verifications.

Faulty Incubator Fan

Using Service Software, initialize the Incubator. To replicate the issue, turn the fan on.

Replace the fan.

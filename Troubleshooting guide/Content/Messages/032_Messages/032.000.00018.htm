Technical Support

Troubleshooting

  • Restart the instrument.

  • This error is unlikely to re-occur on the same incubator.

  • No dispatch needed.

Possible Root Cause

Instrument anomaly or “ghost error”. Engineering thinks it is from firmware/instruments setup process. The parameter number is well above the MDD table.

Resolution

Restart the instrument and keep running.

Incubator Fan Speed Is Out of Range

Error Codes

Dec Hex Module

032.001.00117

20.01.75

High Temp (Inc. 1)

033.001.00117

Transition (Inc. 2)

AMP (Inc. 3)

Complaint Description

  • High Temp (Incubator 1) Fan speed is out of range.

  • Transition (Incubator 2) Fan speed is out of range.

  • AMP (Incubator 3) Fan speed is out of range**.

Technical Support

Auto Dispatch Fan Speed Errors as a Severity 2. Troubleshoot error as normal. Inform customers FSEField Service Engineer. will contact them to schedule service. Customer can continue to process samples if instrument restart in successful.

Troubleshooting

  • Restart the instrument.
  • Wait for about an hour to see if the error repeats during start-up.

Possible Root Causes

  • Faulty or misconnected fan.

Field Service

Fan Cable and Connections

  1. Remove the Mid Bay Cover to expose the Incubator Main PCB(s).
  2. Inspect internal fan cable connection at Main PCB.
  3. Remove the Incubator cover and inspect internal fan cable for damage or defects.

Resolution

  • Ensure the internal fan is connected to the “PWR fan” port on the Main PCB.

    **If affecting the AMP Incubator, ensure External Fan is NOT connected to “PWR fan” on Main PCB.

  • Replace fan if cable or connectors found to be damaged or defective.

Test

  • Perform an error-free System Level Operational Qualification (OR).

  • In System Software GUIGraphical User Interface – allows users to interact with the software using images, graphical icons, and visual indicators rather than text commands., verify Incubator temps are within specification.

    Module Temp (Range)
    High Temp (Inc. 1) 64°C (+/- 0.5°C)
    Transition (Inc. 2) 43.7°C (+1.0° / -0.5°C)
    AMP (Inc. 3) 42.7°C (+/- 0.3°C)

Incorrect Fan Installed

  1. Remove the Mid Bay Cover to expose the Incubator Main PCB(s).
  2. Remove the Incubator cover to inspect the fan part number label:
    1. High Temp (Incubator 1) should read PMD2412PTB1-A (11.8W) (#902924)
    2. Transition (Incubator 2) should read PMD2412PTB3-A (6.7W) (#902925)
    3. AMP (Incubator 3) should read PMD2412PTB3-A (6.7W) (#902925)

Resolution

Remove and replace with correct fan for appropriate Incubator.

Test

  • Perform an error-free System Level Operational Qualification (OR).

  • In System Software GUI, verify Incubator temps are within specification.

    Module Temp (Range)
    High Temp (Inc. 1) 64°C (+/- 0.5°C)
    Transition (Inc. 2) 43.7°C (+1.0° / -0.5°C)
    AMP (Inc. 3) 42.7°C (+/- 0.3°C)

Fan Bearings

  1. Remove the Mid Bay Cover to expose the Incubator Main PCB(s).
  2. Remove the Incubator cover to manually inspect the fan impeller movement for friction due to corroded bearings:

Resolution

  • If friction is observed during impeller movement, bearings may be corroded and fan should be replaced.
  • Replace with correct fan for associated incubator:
    • High Temp (Incubator 1) – PMD2412PTB1-A (11.8W) (#902924)
    • Transition (Incubator 2) – PMD2412PTB3-A (6.7W) (#902925)
    • AMP (Incubator 3) – PMD2412PTB3-A (6.7W) (#902925)

Test

  • Perform an error-free System Level Operational Qualification (OR).

  • In System Software GUI, verify Incubator temps are within specification.

    Module Temp (Range)
    High Temp (Inc. 1) 64°C (+/- 0.5°C)
    Transition (Inc. 2) 43.7°C (+1.0° / -0.5°C)
    AMP (Inc. 3) 42.7°C (+/- 0.3°C)

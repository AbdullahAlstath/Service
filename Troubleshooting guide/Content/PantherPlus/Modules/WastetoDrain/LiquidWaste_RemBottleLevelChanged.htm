Liquid Level of Removable Bottle Has Changed

Error Code

Dec Hex

019.000.00036

0x13.0x00.0x0024

Complaint Description

Liquid Waste Module: Liquid level of removable bottle has changed.

Waste-to-Drain is disabled. Panther reverts to manual liquid disposal only.

Technical Support

  • Shutdown and restart instrument.

  • Dispatch FSEField Service Engineer..

Field Service

Ask the customer how full removable waste bottle is when emptied manually.

  • Could be the waste-to-drain straw not seated properly: straw too short or long, causing residual fluid to remain.

  • Damaged straw also prevents bottle from fully evacuating.

Does the waste to drain pump work, and is it pumping effectively? Note: 1800 seconds = 30 minutes.

Test in service software:

  1. Empty removable waste bottle, and check sensor reading as shown below.

  2. Fill the removable waste bottle, remove the cap, and enable waste to drain pump. Watch the bottom float, and check the sensor again.

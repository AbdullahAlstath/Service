Dec Hex

Complaint Description

10/24/2022 17:20:59 (24 13:20:59) [E] (Critical , High ) [MtuExpansion] MTU Sidecar Lift Drive Target (3) not reached, stopped at 178 (AutomaticModuleEvent) [0x53.0x03.0x000A] bytes: 53 00 03 03 0A 03 B2 FF 6E FF [083.003.00010]

Interference with Counterbalance

Resolution

Is the failure occurring at approximately the same spot? Maybe +/- 10 steps, indicating something is challenging movement.

Check the counterbalance and its track for any obstruction or interference, and remove the foreign object.

Case 1

Failed while moving carrier from storage #3 to Loading Position.

Error from Log Files:

2023-05-05 04:38:53,538 [StSerClient:COP->PC] MessageHandler RS232 NormalCOP->PC  53 23 03 01 B3 53 00 03 03 0A 03 E8 29 53 29 53 29

Errored out moving carrier from Loading Position to Storage #3:

  • 53 - 53 – MTU Expansion Unit

  • 23 - 23 – Move Carrier

  • 03 - 03 - Fatal

  • 01 - 01 – Subassembly Sidecar Motion Control

  • B3 - B3 – Sub Module Fatal Error Occurred

00 – Position A (Loading Position)

03 – Position B (Storage Position 3)

Description

While servicing because of a hardware failure due to an MTU jam I observed that the Lift drive rubs against the metal plate at the bottom/front of the MTU loading drawer of the sidecar. When the scissor drive attempts to pick up a carrier at the loading position you can hear a metal-on-metal rubbing. Sometimes, while in service mode, it would jam because of the contact. You can see the wear from the rubbing on the lift drive.

I could not see any way of adjusting the scissor drive to allow for clearance when it extends to pick up a carrier at the loading position. The plate in question (at front bottom of the sliding drawer) has two screws at the bottom but it also has pins that keep it aligned with no play to allow for adjustment. I was having trouble reteaching the loading position because the Lift kept hitting the drawer plate causing a failure of the teaching. This was until I realized that there is a tiny bit of play in the drawer when closed. If the drawer is slammed shut the plate interferes with the lift. If however, you pull back on the drawer slightly after closing, this gives enough clearance to allow for teaching and for the Lift to pick up a carrier without jamming (you often still hear the rubbing though).

Resolution

There was not enough clearance for the scissors to go into the MTU loading drawer. This clearance has to do with the latch position. The latch adjustment is very critical. The optimal clearance is about 1/8” inch between the scissors and the edge of the opening of the MTU loading drawer.

We used an Allen wrench set as gage, positioning the key in between the latch and the plate behind the latch, to get to the right position of the latch, so that when the drawer is closed, the drawer is flush with the sensor plate. Refer to second image below. Maximize the gap between the scissors and the drawer opening space for the scissors to move into drawer. We tried Allen keys from 1.5mm to 5mm. The one that worked the best was 3mm.

Bottom view, to show sensor plate and the MTU loading drawer above it.

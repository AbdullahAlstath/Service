Field Service

COP->PC 53 00 03 05 0A 21 D8 FD 6E FE

11/13/2020 08:12:26 (13 01:12:26) [E] (Critical , High ) [MtuExpansion] MTU Sidecar Scissors Drive Target (33) not reached, stopped at 216 (AutomaticModuleEvent) [0x53.0x05.0x000A] bytes: 53 00 03 05 0A 21 D8 FD 6A FE [083.005.00010]

Sensor Is Obstructed

Check for an object, such as a tiplet, obstructing the sensor.

Check that carrier is fully seated on loading bay drawer or on scissor.

Resolution

Remove obstruction.

Clean sensor if necessary.

Test

Reset and perform MTU Expansion OQ X2.

Poorly Placed Carrier

Check that the carrier is fully seated and seated correctly on pins of the scissors or loading bay drawer.

Check for any obstruction, such as cables out of place.

Resolution

Properly seat carrier.

Secure cables as necessary.

Check sidecar alignment to Panther.

Check teaching, and reteach as necessary.

Test

Reset and perform MTU Expansion OQ X2.

MTUs oriented incorrectly.

Bad Scissor Sensor

The Scissor Sensor is incorrectly detecting State changes. This fault mode is usually manifested if the scissor is attempting to push out the side of the MTU expansion sidecar.

Resolution

Replace the Sensor.

Test

Reset and perform MTU Expansion OQ X2.

Misaligned Pins

Picking or placing showed that the carrier was not lining up with the pins, and it was tilting on the way down the pin.

The issue was misalignment in the forward or backward direction, and the hole the back pin is in allows for adjustment in the forward or backward directions. The current position of the back pin in this storage spot was too far forward compared to the other back pins.

Resolution

By turning the nut underneath, I was able to align the pin, which gave me smoother picks and places.

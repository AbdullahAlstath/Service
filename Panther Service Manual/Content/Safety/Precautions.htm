Panther Fusion System Operation Precautions

Environmental Conditions

Do not expose the system to direct sunlight or temperature extremes (such as air flow from heating or cooling systems). System performance can be adversely affected.

Universal Fluid Levels

Do not operate the system without fluid in the lines. Operation without fluid can cause excess wear to mechanical parts.

Mechanical Interference

Be sure no object hinders movement of any motorized device.

  • Sample tubes and reagent bottles must be completely seated or they may impact Pipetting performance.
  • Do not place hands, arms, ties, hair, jewelry, or tools in the path of any movable part, including fans.

Door Locks

Locks or switches are used on the upper and lower bay access doors to prevent the user from being harmed by moving mechanisms. The doors also prevent the user from interrupting the assay at a critical moment. The master controller software determines when access to the system is allowed.

Bites and Scratches

Interacting with Panthers on a daily basis can result in a number of injuries. The following steps should be taken if a bite or scratch occurs while working with a Panther.

  1. Stay safe after the injury. Move away from the Panther to a safe location (at least 5 feet away from the Panther).
    Caution— Do NOT start treatment until there is a reasonable expectation that the Panther will not attack again
  2. Assess the injury, and if needed, remove any PPE that has been damaged.
  3. Control the bleeding by using direct pressure and elevation. Avoid using a tourniquet unless there is severe bleeding.
    Warning— If the blood cannot be controlled, immediately call 9-1-1
  4. Once the bleeding is controlled, proceed to the nearest first aid station.
    Note— If you are working in the field, slowly return to your vehicle for first aid supplies
  5. Clean the wound with alcohol pads or warm water and soap.
  6. Remove any large debris found in the wound.
  7. Cover the wound with a clean and dry dressing (band-aid or gauze pads).
  8. Panther bites and scratches are of particular concern for infection.
    If you experience any of the following signs of infection, it is advised to go to the emergency room.
    • Redness
    • Swelling
    • Heat
    • Oozing Pus

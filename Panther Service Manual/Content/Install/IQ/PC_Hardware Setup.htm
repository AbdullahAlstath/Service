Procedure

Dell OptiPlex XE

  1. Locate the Panther System PC and place it into the PC compartment of the Panther system

  2. Remove plastic and zip ties from USB cables.
  3. Connect the monitor cables to the back of the PC, as indicated in the following photo.

  4. Connect the three USB cables into the USB ports on the back of the PC, as indicated in the following photo.

  5. Connect the Monitor cable into the monitor port on the back of the PC, as indicated in the following photo.

  6. Plug the Panther System PC power cable into the power connector, as indicated in the following photo.

  7. The following photo shows the complete setup of the Panther System PC (OptiPlex XE).

  8. Verify any computer cables will not interfere with the Drawer lock.

  9. Place the Panther System PC power cable in between the designated partition and close the Left Panel Door.

  10. Continue to Imaging the Optiplex XE.

Dell OptiPlex XE2

  1. Locate the Panther System PC and place it into the PC compartment of the Panther system.
  2. Connect the USB devices to the XE2 PC as shown below.
    • Do NOT connect anything to the ports on the front of the PC.
    • Only the XE2's rear USB ports and the USB HUB on the side of the instrument should be used.
    • Devices MUST be connected in the ports as show below.
  3. Verify any computer cables will not interfere with the Drawer lock.

  4. Place the Panther System P power cable in between the designated partition and close the Left Panel Door.
  5. If installing an XE2 with 16GB of RAM [CMP-01711], complete the BIOS Settings before Imaging (SATA RAID On) procedure.
  6. Continue to Imaging the Dell Optiplex XE2.
    7. NOTE—If this is an installation, continue to Software Installation.

Uncrating the Accessory Kit

Parts and Materials Required

  • Cordless drill with Phillips PH2 bit

Time Required

  • 30 minutes

Procedure

  1. Remove the screws labeled OPEN HERE that secure the lid of the Accessory Kit crate.

  2. Remove the three foam blocks surrounding the Canopy and side panels.

  3. Carefully remove the front and rear Canopies.

  4. Remove the box on the left side of the crate that contains the Monitor Arm and Waste Bottle.

  5. Open the box and remove the Monitor Arm and Waste Bottle.

  6. Remove the center foam block from the Accessory Kit crate.

  1. Remove the side trim pieces, instrument cross bar, and the foam blocks that secure them in the crate.

  2. Remove the left and right Canopy panels from the front of the crate. The left Canopy panel is in the inner-most groove and the right Canopy panel is in the front-most groove.

  3. Remove the Bleach Bottle which is located between the Monitor box and the center foam block.

  4. Remove the Panther System PC box from the right side of the Accessory Kit crate.

  5. Open the box and remove the keyboard.

  6. Remove the Panther System PC from the box.
  7. Carefully remove the vertical Accessories box from the center of the Accessory Crate.

  8. Open this box and remove the Pipettor divider, the Hand-Held Barcode Scanner bracket, Hand-Held Barcode Scanner, Corner Trim pieces, DiTi teach caps, Fluid Bottle Caps/Connectors, OQ Cover, and Sample Racks.

  9. Remove the large box located on the right side of the crate.

  10. Open the Monitor box and remove the Monitor.

  11. Remove the left corner cover, USB Hub cover, and Lower Bay Panels along with their supporting foam blocks, from the Accessory Kit crate.
  12. Remove the left corner cover and USB Hub cover from the foam blocks.
  13. Remove the Computer Door, Universal Fluids Drawer Cover and Waste Drawer Cover from the foam blocks.
  14. Remove the Canopy flaps from the slot in the Accessory Kit crate.
  15. Remove the pipettor shield and its standoff foam blocks.

  16. Remove the left and right side covers from the slots at the rear of the Accessory Kit crate.

  17. Remove the system trim pieces from the vertical compartment.
  18. Unwrap the trim pieces.
  19. If requested, save all packing materials in the crate for possible future location. Otherwise, discard all packing material.
  20. Continue to Uncrating the Marketing Kit.

MTU Process for Aptima Assays

This section describes the path MTUs travel within the Panther System during assay processing for AC2 Aptima Combo 2 Assay for Chlamydia trachomatis and Neisseria gonorrhoeae, ACT Aptima Chlamydia trachomatis assay, and AGC Aptima Neisseria gonorrhoeae assay.

  1. An operator loads MTUs into the MTU Multi-tube unit—Container used to process tests in the instrument. An MTU contains five separate reaction tubes. The MTU is moved through the instrument by the linear distributor and includes five tiplets for pipettiing to be used in the mag wash station. Input Queue.

  2. The Distributor removes an MTU from the MTU Input Queue and inserts it into the Sample Dispense Slot where a pipettor dispenses TCR Target capture reagent—An assay-specific reagent added as part of specimen pipetting. and then Sample.

  3. The Distributor removes the MTU from the Sample Dispense Slot and inserts it into the Sample Mix Station where it is mixed.

  4. The Distributor removes the MTU from the Sample Mix Station and inserts it into the High Temperature Incubator.

  5. After an assay-specific incubation period, the Distributor removes the MTU from the High Temperature Incubator and inserts it into the Transition Incubator.

  6. After an assay-specific incubation period, the Distributor removes the MTU from the Transition Incubator and inserts it into the Magnetic Parking Ramp or the Chiller Ramp and then the Magnetic Parking Ramp, depending on assay format.

  7. The Distributor removes the MTU from the Magnetic Parking Ramp and inserts it into the Magnetic Wash. Assay Procedures required to prepare and perform a specific test. In the context of this document, assay refers exclusively to a Hologic test, such as Aptima Combo or Ultrio. specific bead capture and wash steps are performed in order to isolate target capture beads.

  8. The Distributor removes the MTU from the Magnetic Wash module and inserts it into the AMP Load Station. The Reagent Pipettor dispenses AMP reagent into the MTU and mixes the MTU.

  9. The Distributor removes the MTU from the AMP Load Station and inserts it into the High Temperature Incubator.

  10. After an assay-specific incubation period, the Distributor removes the MTU from the High Temperature Incubator and inserts it into the Transition Incubator.

  11. After an assay-specific incubation period, the Distributor removes the MTU from the Transition Incubator and inserts it into the AMP Load Station. A pipettor dispenses Enzyme Reagent into the MTU and mixes the MTU.

  12. The Distributor removes the MTU from the AMP Load Station and inserts it into the Amp Incubator.

  13. After an assay-specific incubation period, the Distributor removes the MTU from the Amp Incubator and inserts it into the HPA Hybridization protection assay—The Hologic HPA technique uses a specific DNA probe, labeled with an acridinium ester detector molecule that emits a chemiluminescent signal. Load Station. A pipettor dispenses Probe reagent into the MTU and mixes the MTU.

  14. The Distributor removes the MTU from the HPA Load Station and inserts into the High Temperature Incubator.

  15. After an assay-specific incubation period, the Distributor removes the MTU from the High Temperature Incubator and inserts it into the HPA Load Station. The Reagent Pipettor dispenses Select reagent into the MTU and mixes the MTU.

  16. The Distributor removes the MTU from the HPA Load Station and inserts it into the High Temperature Incubator.

  17. After an assay-specific incubation period, the Distributor removes the MTU from the High Temperature Incubator and inserts it into the Transition Incubator.

  18. After an assay-specific incubation period, the Distributor removes the MTU from the Transition Incubator and inserts it into the Chiller Ramp.

  19. The Distributor removes the MTU from the Chiller Ramp and inserts it into the Luminometer. LCMP Pumps dispense 200 µL of Auto Detect 1 and 2 into the MTU.

  20. After processing is complete, the Distributor removes the MTU from the Luminometer and inserts it into the Output Queue. The Peri-Pump dispenses deactivation fluid into the MTU and aspirates all fluid from the MTU.

  21. The Output Queue moves the MTU into the Solid Waste Container.

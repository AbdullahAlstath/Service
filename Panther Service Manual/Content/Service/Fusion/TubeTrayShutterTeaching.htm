Reaction Tube Tray Shutter Teaching Procedure

Parts and Materials Required

  • Proper PPE
  • Four (4) full Cap & Vial trays (consumables)

Time Required

  • 30 Minutes

Procedure

  1. Start Service Software and from the SC-Front or F-Front&Loading tab, initialize the Cap and Vial Drawers.
  2. After successful initialization, place Cap and Vial Trays into all four positions of the Left and Right drawers and close the drawers.
  3. Lift the canopy door so that the shutters are visible.
  4. On the Parameter Tab, set the Authorization Code to 1.
  5. On the Teach Shutter tab, execute the Init Shutters command from the command list.
    1. Service Software v4.0.7.0 (w/ System SW v6.2)

    2. Service Software v5.0.12.0 (w/ System SW v7.1)

  6. Select the drawer and column to be taught from the Select Position window.
    1. Select Teach both motors to selected position then select Execute.
  7. Select and execute the Drawer and Column combination to be taught. Under Save Current Axis Position / Get Current Pos., select Get.
  8. Visually inspect the alignment of the shutter openings to the columns of caps and vials on the trays.
    The shutters should be centered on the column(s), providing equal clearance on either side of the caps and vials.
    1. To adjust a shutter, select either Motor 1 (top shutter, motor furthest from the front of the system) or Motor 2 (bottom shutter, closest to the front of the system) from the Select Drive window.
    2. Use the arrows to move the shutter Left / Right until it is aligned with the target columns.
    3. Once the position of the shutter(s) is determined, press the Save/Save All or Save & Verify button.
      Note—Only consider the alignment of trays in the selected drawer (Left or Right); the other drawer will be adjusted separately. However, do consider the alignment of both trays within the same drawer. Try to adjust the shutters as best as possible to best accommodate all columns of both trays.
  9. Complete alignment and teaching for all columns of both drawers.
  10. If any shutters were adjusted, execute the Overwrite NVM command to ensure transfer of the positions to firmware.

Verification

  1. IF any shutters were adjusted, perform a Side Car Pipettor OQ Test.

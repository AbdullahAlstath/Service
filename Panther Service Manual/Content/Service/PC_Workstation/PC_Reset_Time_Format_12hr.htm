Changing the Time Format to 12-Hour

Parts and Materials Required

  • None

Time Required

  • 5 minutes

Procedure

  1. From the FSE Shield, open Windows Explorer.

  2. Right-click on This PC and select Properties.

  3. Select Control Panel.

  4. Select Clock, Language, and Region.

  5. Select Date and Time.

  6. Select Change date and time....

  7. Select Change calendar settings.

  8. Close the Date and Time and Date and Time Settings windows.

  9. Change the highlighted values on the Time tab as shown and select Apply.

  10. Verify the time appears in the 12-hour format.

 

Click the button at the top of the page to send feedback, comments, or change requests.

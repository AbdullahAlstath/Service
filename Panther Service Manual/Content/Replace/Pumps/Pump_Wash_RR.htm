Wash LCMP Removal and Replacement

Parts and Materials Required

  • Proper PPE
  • Hex key, 3 mm
  • PUMP (WASH), LCM 5ML

Time Required

  • 30 minutes

Removal Procedure

  1. Put on proper PPE.
  2. Locate the Wash LCMP and unscrew the pump fittings.

  3. Using a 3 mm hex key, loosen, but do not remove, the four 3 mm screws that secure the pump.

  4. Slide the pump up and pull carefully until the power connector is exposed.

  5. Remove the connector.

  6. Gently remove the Wash LCMP.
    Note—If the module will be returned, clean the module.

Replacement Procedure

  1. Reverse the removal procedure.

    Note—Replace the inlet fitting when replacing a pump. A cross threaded fitting can damage the fitting on the pump.

  2. Verify that connections are properly seated and screws are all in place.
  3. If installing a new LCMP, verify that the DIP switches are properly set, all to the OFF positions.
    DIP SwitchWash Pump
    1OFF
    2OFF
    3OFF
    4OFF
  1. Install Panther System firmware.

Verification

  1. Calibrate the Wash pump.
    1. Open Service software.
    2. Initialize either of the Magwash Magnetic washer stations.
    3. Select the Sensor tab.
    4. Select Calibrate Wash.
  2. Perform a Magnetic Wash Visual Dispense Verification procedure.
  3. Inspect for leaks.

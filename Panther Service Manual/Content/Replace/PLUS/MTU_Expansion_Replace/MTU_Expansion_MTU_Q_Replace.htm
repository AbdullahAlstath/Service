MTU Expansion MTU Queue Removal and Replacement

Parts and Materials Required

  • Proper PPE
  • MTU Multi-tube unit—Container used to process tests in the instrument. An MTU contains five separate reaction tubes. The MTU is moved through the instrument by the linear distributor and includes five tiplets for pipettiing to be used in the mag wash station. EXPANSION QUEUE
  • Hex key, 4mm, long shaft
  • Hex key, 3mm

Time Required

  • 20 Minutes

Procedure

  1. Put on proper PPE.
  1. Shut down the Panther Instrument and PC.
  2. Remove the Luminometer Injector.
  3. Carefully open the Service Drawer.
  4. Unscrew the Panther front cover.
  5. Set the cover and screws aside for reinstallation.
  6. Unscrew the front screw of the MTU Queue with the 4mm hex key.

    aa

  7. Unscrew the rear screw of the MTU Queue with the 4mm hex key.

  8. Unscrew the center MTU Queue mounting location with a 3mm hex key.

  9. Disconnect the CAN cable from the MTU Queue PCB.

  10. Remove the MTU Queue.
    NOTE—If returning the module, decontaminate the module and complete the COD/OBF/RMA Form [19-02-APX-A].

Replacement Procedure

  1. Reverse the removal procedure.
  2. Verify that connections are properly seated and that screws are all in place.
  3. Load the Panther System firmware to the module.

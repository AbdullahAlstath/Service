Waste on the Fly Bellows Assembly Removal and Replacement

The Bellows Assembly is now a replaceable part. The following procedure includes instructions for removal and replacement.

Parts and Materials Required

  • Waste Bellows Pump, Parts Replacement Kit

Time Required

  • 25 minutes

Procedure

Part A - Prepping Replacement Bellows Pump

The replacement Bellows Pump kit will include additional parts that need to be removed before installation of the Bellows Pump.

  1. Remove the Bellows Pump replacement kit from packaging.
  2. Holding the base of the Bellows, twist to remove the Bellows Pump from the black pump base housing and piston.

    3. Note: If the displacement cup falls out of the bellows, carefully re-insert the displacement cup into the base of the Bellows Pump.

Part B - Removing the Transfer Pump

Note: Ensure the vacuum is ON.

  1. Start Service Software and purge as much of the fluid from the Removeable Waste Bottle with the following.
    1. Select IC-Waste on the Fly.
    2. Select Liquid Waste on the Fly.
    3. Select Init under Transfer Pump.
    4. Select ON under Transfer Pump and run until no fluid is coming into the Removeable Waste Bottle from the Permanent Bottle.
    5. Select OFF under Transfer Pump.
    6. Add approximately 500 mL DI water then repeat Steps 1.a-1.e above.
    7. Select Open under Valve.
    8. After approximately 10 seconds, select Close under Valve.

  2. Turn OFF the Panther instrument.
  3. Remove the Removeable Waste Bottle.
  4. Remove and save the 2 screws holding the Waste Bottle Mounting Block.
  5. Carefully lay the mounting block out of the way to expose the leak sensor plate.
  6. Remove the 3 screws from the plate and move it away from the container to expose the Transfer Pump.

  7. Disconnect the 2 clear fluid lines labeled Bellows Pump, the Transfer Pump connector, and the leak sensor (as needed).

    Note: There will be fluid left in the bellows. Be extremely careful of leaks. Take note of the tubing connection to the pump.


Part C - Replacing Bellows Pump

  1. With the Transfer Pump remove, remove the 2 Phillips screws on top of the Bellows Pump.
  2. Rotate the Bellows Pump out of the pump housing.
  3. Holding the base of the Bellows Pump, rotate the Bellows Pump out of the pump base housing. Dispose of the removed Bellows Pump.
  4. To install the new Bellows Pump housing, rotate the replacement Bellows Pump onto the Bellows Pump base housing.
  5. Rotate the Bellows Pump back into the vertical position and align the Bellows Pump mounting screw holes with the pump housing screw holes.
  6. Secure the replacement bellows to the pump housing with the two Phillips screws.
  7. Re-install the Transfer Pump reversing the procedure in Part A.
  8. With the mounting screws loose, lift the Waste Bottle Block as high as it will travel, then tighten the two screws.
  9. Fully seat the Waste Bottle in the Waste Drawer.
  10. Loosen the two mounting screws and push down on the Waste Bottle.
  11. Allow the Waste Bottle to settle, then fully tighten the mounting screws.

Verification

  1. Perform the Liquid Waste on the Fly Verification Procedure.

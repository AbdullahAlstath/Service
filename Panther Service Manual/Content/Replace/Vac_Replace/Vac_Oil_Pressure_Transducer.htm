Vacuum and Oil Pressure Transducer Replacement

Parts & Materials

  • PPE
  • FSE Toolkit
  • OIL/VACUUM, PRESSURE SENSOR PCB

Time Required

  • 30 minutes for replacing the Vacuum Pressure Transducer
  • 1 hour for replacing the Oil Pressure Transducer

Procedure

Part A - Replacing the Oil Pressure Transducer

  1. Put on proper PPE.
  2. Power off the Panther System.
  3. Disconnect the cable to the Pump Monitor PCB.
  4. Unscrew both Oil Fittings.
  5. Using a 2.5mm hex driver, remove the four screws that secure the Oil Pressure Transducer to the Pumps Module. Discard the Oil Pressure Transducer.
  6. Remove the yellow plug and secure the new Oil Pressure Transducer using the 2.5mm hex driver and the four screws previously removed.
  7. Screw in the Oil Fitting coming from the Oil Pump to the right side of the Oil Pressure Transducer.
  8. Screw in the Oil Fitting coming from the splitter valve to the left side of the Oil Pressure Transducer.
  9. Connect the cable to the Pump Monitor PCB.
  10. Proceed to the Verification section.

Part B - Replacing the Vacuum Pressure Transducer

  1. Put on proper PPE.
  2. Power off the Panther System.
  3. Disconnect the cable to the pump monitor PCB.
  4. Unscrew the Vacuum Fitting.
  5. Using a 2.5mm hex driver, remove the four screws that secure the Vacuum Pressure Transducer to the Pumps Module. Discard the Vacuum Pressure Transducer.
  6. Do not remove the yellow plug and secure the new Vacuum Pressure Transducer using the 2.5mm hex driver and the four screws previously removed.
  7. Screw in the Vacuum Fitting to the right side of the Vacuum Pressure Transducer.
  8. Connect the cable to the pump monitor PCB.
  9. Proceed to the Verification section.

Verification

Part A - Oil Pressure Transducer

  1. Power on the Panther System.
  2. Prime Operation of pumping fluid through tubing to ensure proper and consistent fluid delivery (remove air from the tubing, etc.). the system.
  3. Verify no fluid leaks at the pressure sensor.

Part B - Vacuum Pressure Transducer

  1. Power on the Panther System.
  2. Prime the system.

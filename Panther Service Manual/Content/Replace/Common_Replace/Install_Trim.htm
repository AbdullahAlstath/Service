Trims and Door Panel Assembly

Time Required

  • 30 minutes

Panel Assembly Procedure

  1. Remove the 3 screws along the bottom of the side of the Panther.
  2. Install the black trim support piece. Orient the cutout toward the front of the instrument.
  3. Locate the left back corner and bottom left trim fixtures.
  4. Install the left corner fixture by pressing into the two slotted grooves.

  5. Install the bottom left trim with three rubber pin fixtures.

  6. Perform Steps 1–3 for the right trim and corner fixtures.
  7. Pull out the Waste Drawer.
  8. Place the front cover panel in front of the Waste Drawer and align the brackets with the screw holes.

  9. Tighten the two screws with washers on the left side of the Waste Drawer.

  10. Tighten the two screws, with washers, on the right side of the Waste Drawer.

  11. In addition to the four side screws, you will see an additional screw on the center post. Attach the bracket to the Waste Door with this screw and washer.

  12. Pull out the Universal Fluid Drawer.
  13. In the same manner as the Waste Drawer, align the front panel to the two screws on the left side of the drawer, and the one screw on the right side of the drawer.

  14. Tighten the two screws, with washers, on the left side of the Universal Fluid Drawer.

  15. Tighten the one screw, with washer, located on the right side.

  16. To install the PC panel door, place the panel door alongside the two hinges.

  17. Using the four provided screws, and a hex key, tighten the two screws to the bottom door hinge, and two screws to the top door hinge.
  18. Place the left door panel on the three hinges located on the back left side of the Panther system. Slide the pin of the door hinge onto the door bracket for all three brackets.

  19. To disassemble the Panther system panels, carefully reverse the assembly steps in this procedure.
  20. Continue to Install the Front Marketing Panel.

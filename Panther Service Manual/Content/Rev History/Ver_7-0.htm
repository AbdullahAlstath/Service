TCR Carousel Module Overview

 The TCR Carousel is a rotary mixer carousel that keeps the TCR magnetic particles in suspension. The TCR Carousel also has positions for Target Enhancer Reagent (TER) for the Ultrio Elite Assay. The TER bottles rotate with the carousel, but do not spin around like the TCR bottles. The TCR Carousel has a dedicated barcode reader that ensures correct bottle placement. The TCR Carousel is a rotary mixer carousel that keeps the TCR magnetic particles in suspension. The TCR Carousel also has positions for modifier bottles (TCR-M for the Ultrio Assay. The modifier bottles rotate with the carousel, but do not spin around like the TCR bottles. The TCR Carousel has a dedicated barcode reader that ensures correct bottle placement. The instrument controls access to one slot at a time to coordinate the loading of TCR/modifier with the Reagent Rack.

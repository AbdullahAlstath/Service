Barcode Scanner Information

Reagent Bay and Sample Bay Barcode Scanner Information

All Sample Bay and Reagent Bay bar code scanners are to be replaced on Failure ONLY.
All scanners removed from Instruments must be returned immediately to the Hologic Dx Repair Center to be repaired.

All Panther Fusion Systems require a THIN Sample Bay Bar Code Scanner
THIN Sample Bay Bar Code Scanners are pre-installed (during manufacture) on all Systems Serial #02590 and above.
